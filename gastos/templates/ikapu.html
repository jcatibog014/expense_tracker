How it works

  1. When you add income, {{ percent }}% of it moves by itself from that wallet into your Ikapu wallet. This is the set-aside. It is a transfer, so it doesn't count as spending.
  2. When you give at church, press Give ikapu. It writes down spending from the Ikapu wallet in the Ikapu category.
  3. If you change or delete an income, its set-aside changes with it.
